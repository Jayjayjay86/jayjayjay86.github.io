Is It Organic or Injected?

I built a little tool called OrganicOrNot. It checks if a viral video is actually popular or if someone paid to make it look that way.

You paste a video link. The tool looks at three things: when the views spiked (2 AM is suspicious), who commented (fake accounts are a red flag), and how the growth curve looks (real videos grow gradually, not like a cliff).

You get a simple score from 0 to 100. Low score = probably manipulated. High score = probably real.

I'm just a data nerd who got curious about how this stuff works. I'm not selling anything big – just offering a free sample report if you want to see it.
