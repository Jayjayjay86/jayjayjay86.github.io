Teaching Journey

2000

Humble Beginnings

Started teaching at a tutoring school in Phuket, helped develop it into a leading language institute. Organized large educational events for hundreds of students, developing a unique teaching methodology still used today.

2010

A New Beginning

Moved to the Bangkok metropolitan area to pursue a teaching career. Collaborated with private kindergartens, developed specialized early childhood education curricula, while maintaining academic standards for older students.

2020

Digital Pivot

Developed an online teaching system during lockdowns, created digital exercises and conversation practice systems via Facebook and Line Messenger. Maintained 100% student retention throughout the transition.

2025

Continuing Success

Today blends traditional teaching with modern technology, offering both in-person and online classes. Maintains a track record of academic success while adapting to new challenges.
